Built, Not Bought

The Forge Metaphor

Iron becomes steel through heat, pressure, and time. There are no shortcuts. You can't rush the process. That's exactly how we develop soccer players — and people.

The name "Forge" isn't marketing. It's a philosophy. Every great player we've developed was shaped through patient, intentional coaching, honest relationships, and the willingness to trust the process even when it's hard.

"We grow talent from within. We don't poach players from other clubs, and we don't build rosters by collecting players developed elsewhere."

Development Over Selection

Many clubs build their reputation by selecting already-developed players from other programs and putting them in a jersey. We build ours by developing players from the ground up — from their first session at Forge through their college commitment.

The difference is real, and families feel it. When a coach has watched your player develop over years, their feedback carries weight that no template evaluation ever could.

The Adult in the Room

Youth soccer can be an anxious space. Parents feel pressure to make the "right" choice. Players feel pressure to perform. Coaches sometimes feel pressure to win at the cost of development.

We choose to be the adult in the room. That means honest conversations — including ones that are hard to hear. It means telling a family when their player isn't ready for the Performance pathway, and explaining exactly what they need to work on to get there. It means celebrating when a player earns a spot at an elite platform club, because that's our job done.

Three Core Promises

  • Honest evaluations — We tell you the truth about where your player is and what they need, every time.
  • Personal relationships — Your coach knows your player's name, their town, their other sports, and their goals.
  • Patience with development — We don't write off late bloomers. We build programs around the reality that players develop at different rates.

"Every player who earns a spot at an elite platform club is OUR success story. That's the Forge philosophy in action."

Forge Community — U6 to U12

The Curious Fox Program

Forge Community is where structured development begins. Ages U6 through U12. Our goal at this stage is simple: help your child fall in love with the game.

No scores. No standings. No pressure. Just fun, friends, and a ball at their feet.

What Kids Learn

  • Dribbling and ball mastery — the foundation of everything
  • First touch and passing fundamentals
  • Running with the ball, changing direction
  • Teamwork, listening, and following instructions
  • How to win with grace and lose with dignity
  • Why showing up and working hard matters

How Sessions Work

Sessions are once per week, age-appropriate, and built around small-sided games — 3v3 and 4v4 formats where every child touches the ball constantly. No one is standing on the sideline waiting for a turn.

Our licensed coaches use age-appropriate methodology designed specifically for young children's motor development and attention spans. Sessions are energetic, structured, and always end with a smile.

For Parents

We run parent education workshops alongside our Futures program. You'll learn how to support your young athlete without creating pressure, how to talk about soccer at home, and how to recognize the signs that your child is ready to grow into more competitive play.

"Multi-sport participation is actively encouraged at this age. We want your child to be an athlete first."

No Tryouts. Everyone Welcome.

Forge Futures has no tryouts and no cuts. This program is open to every child in the Fox Valley corridor. Our most affordable option — starting well under $200 per season. Financial assistance is always available.

The Platform Club Myth

What Platform Clubs Are

ECNL, MLS Next, and Girls Academy are national leagues for top-level youth competition. They offer national competition, college recruiting exposure, and high training standards. For players tracking toward Division I, they can offer genuine value.

What They Actually Cost

  • $3,000–$8,000+/year in club fees alone
  • National travel: flights, hotels, rental cars — multiple weekends per year
  • 3–4 practices per week, year-round
  • Mandatory commitment — other sports, family vacations, school events are secondary
  • Roster volatility — players are cut and replaced regularly

The Myth

"My child MUST join a platform club to get recruited for college soccer."

The Truth

College coaches recruit players, not badges. Here's how recruiting actually works:

  • Coaches watch film — a great highlight video gets attention regardless of club
  • Coaches attend showcases — any club can attend top showcases
  • Coaches respond to direct outreach — a well-crafted Sports Recruits profile works
  • Coaches rely on personal relationships — our staff ARE college coaches who can speak to your player directly

Our players earn college offers every single year. Without ECNL. Without MLS Next. Because great coaching, great character, and a real relationship with a coach who knows your name matters more than a national league badge.

When Should a Player Consider a Platform Club?

If your player is genuinely tracking toward D1 and a platform affiliation offers specific exposure or scouting access that meets their goals — that may be the right move. And if it is, we'll help you make that transition and celebrate it. That's our job done.

"Leaving for an elite platform is success, not failure. We say that, and we mean it."

Coaches Who Know Your Name

Active College Coaches on Our Staff

Our staff includes active D1, D3, and community college coaches — people who are currently making recruiting decisions at their schools. They don't just advise players about college soccer. They're in the ecosystem, watching film, making calls, and attending showcases.

When they say a player is ready for the next level, college coaches listen. Because they're peers.

Face-to-Face Evaluations

At a large platform club, player evaluations are often template-driven — a scoring sheet, a number, a form letter. At Forge, evaluations are conversations.

A coach who has watched your player develop sits down with your family and gives direct, specific, actionable feedback. They know your player's strengths, what they need to work on, and where they realistically fit in the pathway. No surprises. No form letters. Real feedback.

Coaching Education Standards

  • All coaches hold required USSF or equivalent licenses
  • Ongoing professional development — coaching never stops learning
  • Age-appropriate periodization training across all levels
  • Transparent evaluation criteria published before tryouts

Unified Curriculum

One of the most undervalued things a club can offer: consistency. At Fox Valley Forge SC, the same philosophy and methodology runs from U6 to U19. Players build on the same technical foundation year after year. When they move up a level, they don't have to relearn the fundamentals — they build on them.

Coach Continuity

We don't churn coaches. When a player builds a relationship with a coach, we protect that relationship. Coaching continuity — staying with the same coach for multiple seasons — is one of the strongest predictors of player development. We build for it intentionally.

"At other clubs, you're a number on a roster. Here, our staff knows your name, your hometown, your sports, and your motivations."

Your Path, Your Pace

The Fear We Hear Most

"What if we make the wrong choice?"

Parents ask us this constantly. What if we pick the wrong level? What if our child develops later than expected? What if they want to try a different sport? What if the all-in commitment is too much?

We built Fox Valley Forge SC to eliminate that fear.

Multi-Sport Athletes

We don't just tolerate multi-sport participation — we actively encourage it. Research consistently shows that multi-sport athletes develop better movement patterns, suffer fewer overuse injuries, and often emerge as stronger athletes by their mid-teens than single-sport specialists who started early.

At Fox Valley Forge SC, playing baseball, basketball, lacrosse, or gymnastics alongside soccer is a feature, not a problem. Our scheduling reflects that commitment — not just our words.

Late Bloomers

The Evaluation Process

We Meet Your Player at Their Level

Our approach is holistic. We work hard to find the right fit for every player — whether that's Forge Ignite, Forge Performance, a training-only option, or another pathway within our organization.

In most cases, we find a home for every player who comes to us. In rare situations — limited roster spots, coaching availability, or age group constraints — we may not be able to offer a placement. We will always be honest about that, and we will always help guide families toward the right next step.

Before Tryouts: Open Evaluation Days

We run open evaluation sessions for players and families who want to experience our coaching before committing to tryouts. No pressure. No obligation. Just come and train with us and see if the fit feels right.

The Evaluation Itself

Our evaluations are face-to-face assessments conducted by coaches who have context — not just a clipboard and a number. We evaluate:

  • Technical ability — ball mastery, first touch, passing, shooting
  • Tactical awareness — decision-making, positioning, reading the game
  • Physical attributes — speed, strength, athleticism for age
  • Character and coachability — we weigh this heavily. Talent without character doesn't get far here.

After Evaluation: Honest Feedback

Every family receives direct, specific feedback from a coach — not a form letter, not a template score. You'll hear what your player did well, what they need to work on, and where we recommend they be placed. These are honest conversations, delivered with respect.

Timeline

  • Annual Ignite & Performance pathway tryouts: Typically April–May for the following fall season. Open to new and returning members.
  • Open evaluation days: Announced in March — sign up for our newsletter to be notified
  • Ignite & Performance Pathways: Rolling placements throughout the year as spots open
  • Returning players: Existing roster members have placement priority

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to join Fox Valley Forge SC?

Costs vary by program. Forge Foxes (U3–U6) is our most affordable entry point. Forge Community and Forge Ignite range from approximately $800–$2,400/year. Forge Performance ranges from $2,200–$2,950/year. As a non-profit, every dollar goes back into player development. Financial assistance is available — just ask.

When are tryouts for the Performance pathway?

Performance pathway tryouts are typically held in April and May for the following fall season. Open evaluation days are announced in March. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified as soon as dates are set.

Can my child play another sport while at Forge?

Absolutely — and we mean that. We actively encourage multi-sport participation, especially for younger players. We build our schedules to accommodate other sports seasons, and we never penalize players for other athletic commitments. Multi-sport athletes are welcome here.

What if my child gets recruited by an ECNL or MLS Next club?

We celebrate it. That is our success. Our job is to develop your player to the point where elite platforms want them. When that happens, we support the transition and they join our growing alumni community. We genuinely mean this — we do not hold players back from what's right for them.

How is this different from park district soccer?

Park district programs are a wonderful introduction to the sport. We pick up where they leave off — licensed coaches, a structured development curriculum, competitive play with real opponents, college pathway support, and year-round development opportunities. Many of our families start in park districts and naturally grow into Forge.

Do you guarantee college scholarships?

No — and any club that does is misleading you. We give your player every tool, connection, and opportunity to pursue college soccer. The outcome depends on the player, the academic fit, and the hard work they invest. We're honest about this because we respect you enough to tell the truth.

What age groups do you have?

We serve players from U4 (ages 3–4) through U19 (age 18), with programs at every level from recreational through college-prep competitive.

Where do you train?

We operate two permanent indoor facilities in the Fox Valley corridor — Batavia, Geneva, and St. Charles area — plus outdoor field access across the region. Year-round training with permanent, dedicated facility access.

How do I get started?

Click "Request Information" at the top of this page, or send us a message to arrange a facility visit or ask questions. Our coaches respond personally. We'd love to show you around.

Is Forge really a non-profit organization?

Yes. Every dollar collected goes directly back into player development, facility quality, coaching education, and program support. No shareholders, no profit motive — just the mission. This is one of the most meaningful ways we differ from many of our competitors.

What are the US Soccer birth-year changes and how do they affect my player?

US Soccer recently shifted from birth-year age groups to a grade-year model aligned with the school calendar. This means age groups are now based on your child's school grade rather than their birth year. In practice, the change simplifies scheduling for families (soccer seasons align with school years) and better reflects a child's physical and social development stage. If your player was mid-program during the transition, we will work with you directly to ensure correct placement in the right group. Questions? Email us — we're happy to walk through exactly how this affects your family.

Why did Campton United and Fox Valley Strikers merge to form Forge?

This was a merger by choice, not necessity — two healthy, well-established clubs deciding to lead together rather than compete separately. Combined, our two organizations bring 78 years of Fox Valley youth soccer history. The merger created the region's largest player-centric club with two permanent indoor facilities, a unified coaching staff, and a single development pathway from U3 through college prep. Families who were with Campton United or Fox Valley Strikers will find the same coaches, the same community values, and a bigger platform to grow in. We are one valley, one club — and this was the right move for the players we serve.
