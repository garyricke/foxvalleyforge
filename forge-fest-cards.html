What each card back captures

  • Players — Position, Years Playing, Previous Club, Favorites (Movie · Athlete · Team · Food · Song) and a Character Tenet stamp.
  • Coaches · Directors · Staff — Role details (Coaching Since, License, Background) replace the youth stats; Favorites + Tenet stay, so every card reads as one set.
  • Board — Role, Years With Club and a "Why Forge" line, plus Favorites + Tenet.
  • The director cards double as social announcement graphics — the front alone crops cleanly to a square or 4:5 post.

Open — needs a decision from Mike or Bridgett

  • Director & Board colours. Bridgett assigned three; Mike wants five distinguishable. I've proposed Bronze for Directors and Graphite for Board — both hold up in flat CMYK next to the other three. Swap on request.
  • Coach & director photos — note these are NOT illustrated. Players get the semi-realistic redraw; adult cards stay photographic (see Chad Ransom's sample above — a real headshot worked up into a sideline action shot). Because there's no redraw to forgive a weak source, the photo matters more here than it does for the kids. Best case is a genuine touchline action shot. Failing that, a shoulders-up phone photo works: good even light on the face (outdoors in shade is ideal), plain-ish background, eyes to camera, no hat or sunglasses, held at eye level rather than below, and sent at full resolution (AirDrop or "Actual Size" in email — not pasted into a document). A posed selfie at arm's length is fine. Every adult sees their own card before it prints.
  • Still needed: photos for Jeff Muhr, Greg Muhr, Angel and Taylor. Karim and Colleen are in hand. Greg, Angel and Taylor have never actually been asked — the 28 Jul request only went to Jeff and Colleen.

A player with no submission still gets a card; an adult who isn't on a list gets nothing.
