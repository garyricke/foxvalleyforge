What each card back captures

  • Players — Current Position, Years Playing Soccer, Previous Club, plus Favorites (Movie, Athlete, Team, Food, Song) and a Character Quality crest (Integrity · Perseverance · Indomitable Spirit · Respect).
  • Coaches / Directors / Staff — Role details (Coaching Since, License, Playing Background) replace the youth stats; Favorites + Character Quality stay for a consistent set.
  • Board — Years With Club, Role, "Why Forge" line, plus Favorites + Character Quality.
  • Color code — Navy = players & coaches · Crimson accent = directors · Gold = board.
  • Once you pick a direction, I'll turn each card slot into an exact survey question so the form captures precisely what the template needs.
