The Forge Cup logo

Fox Fire, the approved mark for the Forge Cup: one flame in the club's crimson and navy, with a fox looking up at the ball. Every version below is ready to download.

For the GotSoccer registration site

Use the square mark. It's 1000 × 1000 pixels on a white background, which is the shape registration sites crop logos to. A transparent version and the wide horizontal logo are below if the site asks for them.

Every version

PNG files have transparent backgrounds for the web, social and registration sites. SVG files are vector, so printers and sign shops can scale them to any size.

Notes

Sponsor line

The "Powered by" swoosh from the concept is left off. Nike's logo can only appear with their permission, so we'll add a sponsor line once a partnership is confirmed and the sponsor sends approved artwork.

Using it

Keep clear space around the mark at least the width of the ball. On navy or photos, use the reversed versions: they carry a white outline so the navy half of the flame stays visible.

Colors & type

Club colors and Oswald, the club's headline typeface.
