The Forge Cup: logo concepts

Karim's design, interpreted three ways by two image models, Google Gemini and OpenAI. Each was given his emblem and the club's three colors.

Karim's original

The starting point: a trophy whose rim forms fox ears, a ball in the cup, set on an anvil. Every concept below is built from this.

1 · Refined

Same concept, cleaned up: symmetry, consistent outlines, a more balanced anvil.

2 · Crest

The emblem inside a shield with the name on it, made for patches, medals and jerseys.

3 · Modern minimal

Fewer pieces, bolder shapes, so it still reads at app-icon or sleeve size.

These are raw model outputs. The chosen direction gets redrawn as clean vector artwork before it goes to print or GotSoccer.
