The Opportunity

More Than a Logo Wall

The reference page (Wasco Athletics) is the common approach: a tidy grid of logos that link out. It's fine — but it treats sponsors as decoration. We can make the Forge sponsor page a genuine value platform that partners are proud to be on and want to renew.

Typical Sponsor Page

The Forge Sponsor Page

1

The Logo Standard — Every Logo, Pixel-Perfect

Your non-negotiable, and rightly so. A sponsor page is only as premium as its worst logo. Every partner logo will be delivered in a razor-sharp format that stays crisp at any size — cleaned up, or fully redrawn, when what we're given isn't good enough.

The Rule

No blurry PNGs, no jagged edges, no off-brand colors. If a sponsor hands us a low-res logo pulled off a business card, we redraw it so it's crisp at any size — from a favicon to a stadium banner.

STEP 1 · SOURCE

We find the best file

Gather the sharpest available logo — from the sponsor or public sources. Their effort: none.

STEP 2 · ASSESS

Triage quality

Already clean vector → just normalize. Anything blurry or low-res → clean it up.

STEP 3 · REDRAW

Redraw service or AI

Rebuild it crisp with sharp edges and brand-accurate color, in a stays-sharp-at-any-size format.

STEP 4 · APPROVE

Sponsor signs off

Zoom-test for sharpness, then the sponsor reviews and approves before anything goes live.

How We Clean Up Every Logo

Two tools, matched to the logo — a professional redraw service ($14/logo) and AI. No in-house hand-drawing. My recommended default is the highlighted row.

Best-Practice Build

How We'll Build It

Engineered so adding the 30th sponsor is as easy as the 1st — and so it stays fast, accessible, and on-brand.

Easy to add sponsors

Every sponsor lives in one simple list, so the page, spotlights, and homepage banner all stay in sync. Adding a new sponsor is quick and clean — no rebuild.

Fast, reliable hosting

Every photo and logo lives on a high-speed content-delivery platform and is automatically sized for the web — so pages load fast on any phone or computer.

Quick & easy to use

Images load only as needed, everything works by touch and keyboard, and text stays easy to read — so the page is quick and usable for everyone.

Search-engine friendly

Each sponsor is set up so Google and other search engines clearly understand their business — a little extra visibility, at no extra effort.

Mobile-first

Most visitors are on phones. Grid, banner, and spotlights are designed for touch first, then scaled up.

On-brand & easy to run

Forge navy/gold/crimson throughout. Adding or updating a sponsor is a small, well-scoped task under the maintenance plan.

What the Sponsor Has to Do

Almost Nothing

The whole point: keep the steps on the sponsor's side minimal — ideally none. We source and build everything from the best available info; the sponsor simply reviews the finished spotlight and gives the OK.

We source the logo — from the sponsor or public sources
We clean it up crisp — a redraw service or AI
We source & enhance a photo — the storefront or a hero image
We write the "about" — short and factual, from public info
We gather address, hours & phone — plus the map
We add the website link and build the spotlight
We host every photo & logo on a fast, reliable platform
We put it on the banner and keep it up to date
The Sponsor's Only Step

Review and approve. Every logo, photo, and detail is sent for a final OK before it goes live — nothing publishes without their sign-off. If there's ever one thing we genuinely can't source, we'll ask for that single item and nothing more.

Proposed Plan

Phases

Sequenced so we can start the moment Mike's sponsor list arrives, and launch in a clean, reviewable order.

Phase 0

Get the list

Mike sends the sponsor list — names and websites are enough. We start sourcing logos, photos, and details for each.

Phase 1

Logo pipeline

Clean up every logo to a crisp, stays-sharp-at-any-size format, and set up one shared, fast-loading home for all sponsor images.

Phase 2

Sponsor page + spotlights

Build the even logo grid and the mini-home-page spotlights, all from one simple list — and set up to be search-engine friendly from the start.

Phase 3

Homepage banner

Add the auto-scrolling logo banner to the bottom of the homepage, wired to the same sponsor data.

Phase 4

Sponsor approvals

Send each finished spotlight for review, make any tweaks, and collect the OK — nothing goes live without it.

Phase 5

Final check & launch

Zoom-test every logo, check phones, links, and readability, then publish the approved sponsors.

This is a substantial build; it fits naturally under a pre-pay maintenance block, with per-sponsor logo work billed as it comes in. We'll scope hours once the sponsor count is known.
