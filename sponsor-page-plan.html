The Opportunity

More Than a Logo Wall

The reference page (Wasco Athletics) is the common approach: a tidy grid of logos that link out. It's fine — but it treats sponsors as decoration. We can make the Forge sponsor page a genuine value platform that partners are proud to be on and want to renew.

1

The Logo Standard — Every Logo, Pixel-Perfect

Your non-negotiable, and rightly so. A sponsor page is only as premium as its worst logo. Every partner logo will be delivered as clean vector (SVG) — redrawn from scratch when what we're given isn't good enough.

The Rule

No blurry PNGs, no jagged edges, no off-brand colors. If a sponsor hands us a low-res logo pulled off a business card, we redraw it so it's crisp at any size — from a favicon to a stadium banner.

STEP 1 · INTAKE

Collect the best source

Ask for true vector first (AI / EPS / SVG / PDF). Accept raster only as a fallback.

STEP 2 · ASSESS

Triage quality

Clean vector → normalize. Simple raster → trace. Complex / low-res → manual redraw.

STEP 3 · VECTORIZE

Redraw as needed

Rebuild curves, letterforms and brand-accurate colors. Export light, dark & mono SVGs.

STEP 4 · QA + LIBRARY

Zoom-test & store

Verify sharp at 3× zoom, then save the master to a shared Forge sponsor asset library.

Vectorization Options — Beyond Sticker Mule

Sticker Mule works, but it's one tool for every job. Best practice is to match the method to the logo — and keep a manual-redraw path for the hard ones. My recommendation is the highlighted row.

Vectorizer.AI, Vector Magic, Illustrator Image Trace		 Simple, high-contrast logos only OK — but bloated anchor points & jagged curves; always needs cleanup Cheap · fast

Illustrator / Figma, by us		 The default for anything detailed, low-res, or off-brand Best — true reconstruction, full control, no per-logo fee, keeps assets in-house Billed as maintenance · same-day for most
Redraw services
Sticker Mule, VectorWiz, freelance pros		 Overflow, or unusually complex crests High — professional hand-redraw ~$15–30/logo · 24–48h

Why not auto-trace everything: auto-traced files carry 10–100× more anchor points than needed, with jagged curves and broken text — the reason print shops and embroiderers reject them. Fine for a simple wordmark; wrong for a detailed crest. Keeping an in-house redraw path guarantees the standard on every logo, not just the easy ones.

Why Sponsors Win

This is the pitch that helps the club close and renew sponsors — because the page gives them things a logo grid never could.

Best-Practice Build

How We'll Build It

Engineered so adding the 30th sponsor is as easy as the 1st — and so it stays fast, accessible, and on-brand.

One data source

Every sponsor is one entry (name, tier, logo, colors, offer, links, photos). The page, modals, and homepage banner all render from it — no duplication, no code edits to add a sponsor.

Assets on Cloudinary

Sponsor photos and video hosted & auto-optimized on Cloudinary (our standard); logos as lightweight SVG. Fast on every device.

Fast & accessible

Lazy-loaded below-the-fold logos, keyboard-navigable modals, alt text, and strong contrast — the performance discipline we just applied site-wide.

SEO structured data

Each sponsor marked up as a LocalBusiness so search engines understand them — extra discoverability value baked in.

Mobile-first

Most visitors are on phones. Grid, banner, and spotlights are designed for touch first, then scaled up.

On-brand & easy to run

Forge navy/gold/crimson throughout. Adding or updating a sponsor is a small, well-scoped task under the maintenance plan.

Proposed Plan

Phases

Sequenced so we can start the moment Mike's sponsor list arrives, and launch in a clean, reviewable order.

Phase 0

Align & intake

Confirm sponsorship tiers with Mike, finalize the intake checklist/form, and gather the full sponsor list & assets.

Phase 1

Logo pipeline

Vectorize every logo to the standard, build light/dark/mono variants, and stand up the shared sponsor asset library.

Phase 2

Sponsor page + spotlights

Build the data model, the tiered logo grid, and the rich spotlight modals from a single source.

Phase 3

Homepage banner

Add the auto-scrolling logo banner to the bottom of the homepage, wired to the same sponsor data.

Phase 4

Reporting & SEO

Hook up per-sponsor view/click tracking and LocalBusiness structured data.

Phase 5

QA & launch

Zoom-test every logo, check mobile, accessibility, and links, then go live and share proofs with each sponsor.

This is a substantial build; it fits naturally under a pre-pay maintenance block, with per-sponsor logo work billed as it comes in. We'll scope hours once the sponsor count is known.
