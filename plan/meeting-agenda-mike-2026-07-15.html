don't skip

4 · Trading Cards — Discovery

15 min

Don't quote in the room. Ask, then send a number.

  • Physical print, digital, or both? Print pulls in a vendor, proofs, shipping, and a real budget.
  • How many players and teams? Cards scale linearly — this is the single biggest cost driver.
  • Photos: is there a photo day? Who shoots it? Do usable player photos already exist?
  • Design: front and back? Stats, bio, sponsor logo on the reverse?
  • Sponsor tie-in: are cards a sponsor-revenue vehicle? If so they connect to the sponsor page — real efficiencies there, worth flagging.
  • Deadline: are these meant to be in players' hands for Aug 22?
If he wants physical cards for kickoff — that's photos, design, proofs, and a print run in five and a half weeks. Very tight. Say so in the room.

10 min

  3. Trading cards — likely a fall deliverable unless he insists on kickoff
