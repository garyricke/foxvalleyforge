One valley. One club. One unforgettable day on the course.

Join us for the very first Forge Golf Classic — a full day of championship golf at Eagle Brook Country Club, one of the Fox Valley's premier private courses. Gather your foursome for lunch off the grill, a shotgun scramble, on-course contests, and a BBQ dinner to close it out. Every registration and sponsorship directly supports Fox Valley Forge SC and the young players we're building this club for.
