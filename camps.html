The opportunity

During the season, everyone trains. Everyone competes. Everyone works within a similar schedule. When the season slows down, a different opportunity opens up.

The chance to work on your weaknesses, sharpen your strengths, build new skills, and come back to the field better prepared than you left it. That's what Your Separation Season is built for.

A focused curriculum and high-level coaching, aimed at the technical, tactical, physical and mental qualities that set players apart.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it open to players outside Forge?

Yes. Forge and non-Forge players are welcome. Forge families can register first, from October 14, and receive preferred pricing. Registration opens to everyone on October 18.

What's included?

Instruction for the full program you choose. The November Development Series is six training sessions. Each December camp is three days, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Do players need to attend every session?

Each program is built as a complete development experience, so players get the most from attending every session, especially in November.

How are players grouped?

November has set training groups and times by age. In December everyone attends the same daily hours and is grouped by age and ability for focused, high-quality instruction.

Can players register for more than one program?

Yes. Each program has its own registration and brings its own focus and drills. Register for November plus at least one December camp and save 10%. Siblings who do the same save an extra 10%.

What should players bring?

Equipment, water and other preparation details will be sent to registered families before each program.
