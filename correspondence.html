Site hosting + maintenance budget

To: Hi Mike

Hi Mike,

Wanted to get you a clear picture of what it takes to keep the site running and cared for this year, plus my recommendation. I put it all on one page so the numbers are fully transparent.

The short version:

  • Hosting: $300/year (~$25/mo). Keeps the site live, fast, and secure. For comparison, a Squarespace or WordPress setup runs in the same range but on slower, heavier infrastructure — our build tested faster than both.
  • Maintenance is a pre-paid block of hours. The bigger the block, the lower the hourly rate ($150/hr down to $75/hr). Hours never expire, and you get a live internal status page showing every bit of work done.

Here’s the full rate ladder — the highlighted tier is my recommendation for the year ahead:

Pre-pay maintenance blocks — larger blocks earn a lower hourly rate.

See the full breakdown →

My recommendation this year: the $4,000 · 50-hour block ($80/hr).

Here’s the reasoning. A typical year of upkeep for a club our size runs roughly 27–50 hours. Normally that’d put us in a smaller block — but the new sponsor & partner page is a larger one-time project on top of regular maintenance. Because we lean on AI for most of the logo cleanup and content, that build runs about 20 hours rather than the 40–50 it might otherwise. Folded in with a normal season of upkeep, the 50-hour block covers everything comfortably with room to spare — and locks in the best hourly rate.

If the sponsor page weren’t in the picture this year, I’d point us to the $2,500–$3,000 range instead. It’s really that project that nudges the recommendation up.

Happy to walk through any of it on a call. Just let me know how you’d like to proceed.
