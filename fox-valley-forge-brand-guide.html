Section 01

Brand Essence

"A unified, community-rooted club with professional standards — designed to develop players, people, and pathways without forcing families into an all-or-nothing youth-sports lifestyle."

Fox Valley Forge SC — Brand Positioning Statement

Mission

Fox Valley Forge SC exists to be the premier, community-rooted performance pathway for the entire Fox Valley corridor. We keep the community DNA that built local soccer, while offering a clear, vertically integrated pathway from first kick to college and beyond.

Core Brand Idea

One Valley. One Pathway. — A unified club that combines heritage and credibility with professionalized coaching and accessible, tiered playing options so families never have to leave the valley to find the right level.

Brand Pillars

Heritage & Credibility

Decades of serving Batavia, Geneva, St. Charles, and surrounding communities. Hundreds of college players, thousands of local kids shaped by the game.

Professional Development

Licensed coaching, unified curriculum, age-appropriate training methodology, and a single style of play across all ages and levels.

Accessible Pathways

Two clear pathways under one roof — Performance and Community-Competitive — so every family finds the right fit without compromise.

Whole-Player Culture

Character, academic support, leadership, and mental health alongside performance. Multi-sport tolerance and flexible expectations for real families.

Market Position

Fox Valley Forge SC occupies a strategic position in the suburban Illinois youth soccer landscape: the elite local choice. We sit between the national-platform super-clubs (MLS Next, ECNL, Girls Academy) and purely recreational park-district programs.

Key Differentiator: Families no longer need to choose between a community club and an elite one. Fox Valley Forge is both — high-level soccer without driving into the city three times a week.

Taglines

  • "One Valley. One Club. One Pathway." — Primary
  • "Homegrown players, national ambitions." — Recruitment
  • "From first kick to college." — Development narrative
  • "Your path, your pace." — Inclusivity messaging

Logo System

The Fox Valley Forge SC identity is built on a shield-based crest system featuring the fox — a symbol of intelligence, agility, and regional identity. The crest conveys strength, tradition, and competitive ambition while the fox grounds the brand in the Fox Valley community.

Primary Logo — With Fox Valley

The primary crest features a fox in profile within a layered shield. "Fox Valley" arches above, "Forge" dominates the center in bold display type, and "SC" anchors the base alongside a soccer ball. This is the default logo for all official communications.

Typography Rules

  • Headlines: Always Oswald, uppercase. Use Bold (700) for primary headlines, SemiBold (600) for subheadings.
  • Body text: Always Source Sans 3, sentence case. Regular (400) for body, SemiBold (600) for emphasis.
  • Never use Oswald for body paragraphs or Source Sans for display headlines.
  • Letter-spacing: Add 0.03–0.08em tracking to uppercase display text. Body text uses default spacing.
  • Line height: 1.1–1.2 for display text, 1.5–1.6 for body text.
  • Fallback stack: Oswald → Impact → sans-serif. Source Sans 3 → Helvetica Neue → Arial → sans-serif.
Section 05

Voice & Tone

Fox Valley Forge SC speaks as a confident, welcoming leader — not a gatekeeper. The voice should feel like a respected coach: clear, encouraging, direct, and invested in each player's individual journey.

Voice Attributes

Confident, Not Arrogant

We know who we are and what we offer. We state it plainly without needing to tear down competitors or oversell outcomes.

Welcoming, Not Passive

We actively invite families in. We don't just say "all are welcome" — we design programs, pricing, and language that prove it.

Professional, Not Corporate

We operate at a high standard but talk like real people. No jargon, no buzzwords, no cold institutional tone.

Player-First, Always

Every message centers the player's experience and growth. Results and badges are outcomes of good development, not ends in themselves.

Tone by Context

Context Tone Example
Recruitment / Marketing Inspiring, aspirational "Your player's next chapter starts here."
Registration / Admin Clear, helpful, warm "Spring registration is open. Here's what you need to know."
Game Day / Results Energetic, proud "What a battle. Proud of every player who wore the shield tonight."
Youth / Rec Programs Fun, encouraging, playful "Lace up, bring a friend, and get ready to have a blast."
College / Pathway Credible, supportive, honest "We prepare players for whatever path fits them best."
Internal / Coach Comms Direct, respectful, unified "Here's the plan for this weekend. Reach out if you have questions."

Do / Don't

Do Say

  • "We develop players for whatever path fits them best."
  • "High-level soccer without driving into the city."
  • "Your path, your pace."
  • "Homegrown players, national ambitions."
  • "Leaving for an elite platform is success, not failure."
  • "One Valley. One Club. One Pathway."

Don't Say

  • "We're the best club in the area."
  • "We compete with ECNL/MLS Next."
  • "Your kid will get a college scholarship."
  • "Elite-level training" (without context)
  • "We merged to survive."
  • "Better than [competitor name]."

Key Messaging Themes

  • Community DNA: We are a Fox Valley institution with decades of local roots.
  • Development over selection: We grow players; we don't just collect them.
  • Pathways, not pressure: Multiple levels under one roof, no all-or-nothing commitment.
  • Whole-player values: Character, academics, leadership alongside skill.
  • Less travel, more quality: Professional standards without the lifestyle burden of national-platform travel.
Section 06

Imagery & Photography

Visual content should reinforce the brand pillars: community, development, and accessible excellence. Photography is the most powerful way to show — not just tell — what Fox Valley Forge SC stands for.

Photography Style

Authentic Action

Capture real training, real games, real celebrations. Avoid overly staged or stock-photo-style poses. Movement, effort, and joy should dominate.

Inclusive Representation

Show the full range of the club: all ages (U4 through U19), all genders, all skill levels. Rec kids and performance players both deserve the spotlight.

Community Moments

Families on the sideline, coaches mentoring, teams bonding, post-game high-fives. These moments are as important as highlight-reel goals.

Fox Valley Identity

When possible, feature recognizable local landmarks, fields, and settings that ground the club in its geography and community.

Guidelines

  • Color grading: Slightly warm, with boosted contrast. Avoid overly cool or desaturated tones.
  • Overlays: Use navy at 50–70% opacity for text placement on photos. Crimson overlays for high-energy moments.
  • Cropping: Favor tight, dynamic crops that emphasize faces and movement. Avoid centering subjects statically.
  • Logo on photos: Place logo in lower-right corner with adequate clear space. Use white or gold version over dark areas.
  • Never use competitor logos, inappropriately branded content, or images that contradict the inclusive, player-first ethos.

Naming Convention

All programs and teams lead with the "Forge" name to maintain brand unity:

  • Forge Futures U6 — Foundation program
  • Forge Community 2016 Boys Blue — Community travel team
  • Forge Performance 2012 Girls — Performance pathway team
  • Forge College Prep — Recruitment advisory program

Unified Identity Rule: Never use legacy organization names (Campton United, Strikers Fox Valley) in any new materials. The merger narrative is forward-looking: "We merged to lead, not to survive."

Section 10

Brand Misuse

Protecting the brand means being clear about what not to do. The following are common violations that dilute the Fox Valley Forge SC identity.

Logo Don'ts

×

Do not stretch or distort the logo proportions

×

Do not rotate or tilt the logo

×

Do not reduce opacity below 80% or use as a watermark

×

Do not change the logo colors or apply filters

×

Do not place the logo on busy or clashing backgrounds

×
Soccer!

Do not add text, shapes, or effects to the logo

General Brand Don'ts

  • Never use legacy names (Campton United, Strikers Fox Valley) in new materials.
  • Never use unapproved fonts as substitutes for Oswald or Source Sans 3.
  • Never mix the Primary and Rec/Kids logos on the same piece.
  • Never use brand colors outside the approved palette (including personal "close enough" matches).
  • Never make promises about college scholarships or professional outcomes in marketing.
  • Never disparage competitors by name in any communication.
  • Never use "elite" without qualifying context — lead with "development" language instead.
