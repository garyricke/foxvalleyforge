Player Trading Cards

Up against the wall

The marquee project — Mike's most excited about it, and nobody else is doing anything like it. Target is to have cards in hand to exchange at Forge Fest (Aug 22), so it has to move fast. Illustrative / AI-stylized art direction confirmed.

  • Volume: ~525 kids + ~15–20 coaches + ~8–10 board ≈ 550 people × 20 cards each ≈ 11,000 cards.
  • Photos: kids already shot at media night (player-pass headshots). Coaches & board still need photos — pro photographer, or Gary shoots + AI-enhances.
  • Card fields: name, photo, team label (e.g. "2026 · 11U" — signals age range without asking age directly), top two preferred positions, favorite pro team, favorite player, favorite food, favorite thing outside soccer, years played.
  • Privacy: keep it fun/authentic; no direct age, no siblings — parents are sensitive about what goes on a distributed card.
  • Survey mechanics: form collects the fun facts; player name maps to the photo filename so cards auto-assemble.
  • GaryStart experimenting with card concepts / illustrative style right away.
  • GaryBuild the player survey/data form within 1–2 days; prep the blast-out.
  • MikeGet with Bridget on player photos + the email/survey solution (send via Sprocket).
